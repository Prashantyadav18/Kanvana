Global Vision

Kanvana Foundation was born at IIT Kanpur with a profound belief: every single tree planted is an act of defiance against climate collapse. We began as a small group of passionate individuals who refused to accept the world's environmental decline without a fight.

Our mission spans beyond tree plantation — we educate communities, inspire youth, reduce urban pollution, and create green corridors that restore the natural balance of our ecosystems. We believe change begins with one person, one tree, one act of courage.
